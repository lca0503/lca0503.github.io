About me

I am a Ph.D. student in the Graduate Institute of Communication Engineering at National Taiwan University, advised by Prof. Hung-yi Lee.

My research focuses on speech and language processing, especially instruction-aware speech retrieval, large audio-language models, and self-supervised speech representations.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science and Information Engineering from NTU in 2023. During my undergraduate studies, I worked with Prof. Hung-yi Lee and Prof. Yun-Nung (Vivian) Chen. I also completed research internships at ASUS and the Institute of Information Science, Academia Sinica.
